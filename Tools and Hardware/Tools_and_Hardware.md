# 🧰 Tools & Hardware Guide

This document catalogs essential tools for building and modifying 3D printers and electronics. We highly recommend investing in quality tools for tasks like soldering and crimping, as they directly impact the reliability of your builds.

*Note: Links provided may be affiliate links that help support Alchemical-3D.*

## 🔌 Soldering & Electronics

A solid electrical connection is critical in 3D printing. Poor joints can lead to failed prints or even fire hazards.

| Tool Name & Link | Why it Matters | Category / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[Pinecil / TS100 Soldering Iron](#)** | A good soldering iron with precise temperature control is mandatory for reliable wiring, PCB work, and installing heat-set inserts. | *Soldering* |
| **[High-Quality Rosin Core Solder](#)** | Makes flowing solder into joints much easier and ensures strong electrical connections. | *Consumable* |
| **[No-Clean Flux Pen / Paste](#)** | Flux cleans oxidation off wires and pads, allowing the solder to wet the surface properly. Crucial for custom wiring harnesses. | *Consumable* |
| **[Silicone Wire (Various AWG)](#)** | Highly flexible and heat resistant, perfect for umbilical cables and components near the hotend. | *Wiring* |
| **[JST-XH / Micro-Fit Crimping Tool](#)** | Proper crimps are far superior to solder for connectors. A ratcheting crimper ensures consistent, secure pin connections to your mainboard. | *Tools* |
| **[Digital Multimeter](#)** | Essential for checking continuity, measuring voltage, and troubleshooting electrical faults before they damage components. | *Diagnostics* |
